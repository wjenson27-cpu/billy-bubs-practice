    How this table plays

    Come-out: 7 or 11 wins the pass line. 2, 3, or 12 loses it. Any other number is the point. Then the pass line wins if the point shows before a 7.

    Don't pass wins on 2 or 3, loses on 7 or 11, and pushes on 12. After a point, it wins on a 7.

    Odds are a fair bet. 3-4-5x means 3 times on 4 and 10, 4 times on 5 and 9, and 5 times on 6 and 8. Lay odds are capped so the win matches that multiple. 5x and 10x are flat multiples, with the matching lay.

    Place pays 9:5 on 4 and 10, 7:5 on 5 and 9, and 7:6 on 6 and 8. Buy pays true odds with a 5% commission. Lay is the other side of a buy, with commission on the amount you can win.

    Come and don't come work like pass and don't pass, after a point is on. Come odds are off on the come-out. Don't come odds are working.

    Low Rolls, High Rolls, Roll 'Em All, and Hard Way All Day are the same bonuses as the bubble table. A 7 before the set is finished loses them.

    Setting the dice does not change a fair roll. Controlled shooter is a practice dial and stays off until you turn it on.
