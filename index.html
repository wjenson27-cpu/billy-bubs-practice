      How this machine works

      One cabinet, two rule sets

      This is a bubble-craps practice screen. Toggle Regular or Crapless without leaving the machine. Switching rules picks up the table and keeps your credits. Rolls are always fair random 1–6, like a real bubble machine — there is no dice picker.

      Regular

      Come-out: 7 or 11 wins Pass; 2, 3, 12 are craps (Pass loses). 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 set a point. Then hit that point before a 7. Don't Pass bars 12 (push).

      Crapless

      Come-out 7 wins Pass. 2, 3, 11, and 12 are points along with 4–6 and 8–10. No automatic craps. Don't Pass / Don't Come are not offered. Place 2 / 3 / 11 / 12 are available.

      Low Rolls / High Rolls / Roll ’Em All — practice pays

      Always working. Numbers count after you post. A 7 before the set completes loses. You may take them down.

      • Low Rolls — 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 before 7. 31 FOR 1 ($31 returned per $1, includes stake)
      • High Rolls — 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 before 7. 31 FOR 1
      • Roll ’Em All — every total except 7 before a 7. 156 FOR 1

      Fixed practice schedule, not a casino progressive. Real machines vary.

      Hard Way All Day (side wager)

      Lives between Come and Don't Come. Separate from the single Hard 4/6/8/10 bets. Wins only if Hard 4, Hard 6, Hard 8, and Hard 10 all appear (matching dice) before a 7. Soft/easy 4, 6, 8, or 10 do not count and do not lose the bet. A 7 before the set is complete loses. 165 FOR 1 ($165 returned per $1, includes stake → $164 profit). Dealer Envy is not offered in this practice build.

      Single hardways, Horn, C, and E

      Hardways sit left in a 2×2 (Hard 6 & 10 over Hard 4 & 8). Center is Big Red (any 7, no seven-dice art — table etiquette). Horn legs are the right 2×2. Pass Line and Don't Pass run along the bottom of the felt. Hard 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 stay up until a hard win (paid, bet stays), a 7, the easy way, or you take them down. One-roll props repeat after a win like a machine.

      • Hard 6 / Hard 8 — 9:1 · Hard 4 / Hard 10 — 7:1
      • Big Red — any 7. 4:1
      • Aces (2) / Midnight (12) — 30:1 on the winning dollar
      • Ace-deuce (3) / Yo (11) — 15:1
      • C · Any Craps — 2, 3, or 12. 7:1
      • E · Eleven (Yo) — 11 only. 15:1
      • Horn 4-way — 2, 3, 11, 12. A $4 unit: 2/12 pay 30:1 on the $1 that hits (net +$27); 3/11 pay 15:1 (net +$12). Posted in $4 units.

      Buy vs Place

      Each point number has Buy above and Place below. Both stay up after a hit and lose on 7. Off on the come-out unless you check “Place & Buy work on come-out.” BETS OFF also parks Place and Buy so a 7 or number hit neither pays nor takes them down.

      • Place — 4/10 = 9:5 · 5/9 = 7:5 · 6/8 = 7:6. Crapless Place 2/12 = 11:2 · 3/11 = 11:4.
      • Buy — true odds: 4/10 = 2:1 · 5/9 = 3:2 · 6/8 = 6:5. Crapless Buy 2/12 = 6:1 · 3/11 = 3:1.
      • Buy vig is 5% of the buy stake, taken on a win only (not charged when you post, and not charged if the bet loses). Example: $20 Buy 4 pays $40 true odds minus $1 vig → $39 profit; the $20 stays up.

      Bets Off

      BETS OFF is a machine-style toggle. Pressed and lit means Place and Buy are off: they do not win or lose on the next 7 or number hit, and the chips stay on the layout until you turn them back on. Same idea as calling “bets off” on a live table.

      • Covered: Place and Buy on every number (Regular 4–6 / 8–10; Crapless also 2, 3, 11, 12).
      • Not covered: Pass, Don't Pass, Come, Don't Come, Field, hardways, Hard Way All Day, Big Red, Horn, hops, C, E, Low/High/All. Those still win and lose as usual.
      • Come-out still treats Place/Buy as off unless you check “Place & Buy work on come-out” and Bets Off is not lit.

      Billy’s Way

      BILLY’S WAY (renamed from Across) posts the selected chip as Buy on 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 11, and 12 (whichever your rules offer — Crapless includes 2/3/11/12; Regular buys 4, 5, 9, 10), and Place on 6 and 8 at chip × 6/5 (e.g. $5 chip → Buy $5 on the buys, Place $6 on 6 and on 8).

      Each press stacks one unit on those spots. Numbers you cannot afford are skipped. A toast lists what posted and any shortfall.

      Reset to starting bet

      The machine snapshots your layout on the first come-out roll with bets up this hand. That is the starting bet. A new hand (and a new snapshot) begins after a seven-out, a new session, or a rules switch.

      RESET takes down extra removable bets and re-posts that starting set, skipping anything locked or too expensive for the current credits and phase. It is not REPEAT: Repeat is the previous roll’s set; Reset is the opening layout of the hand.

      Repeat last bet

      REPEAT re-posts the last committed wager set — the bets that were on the layout when you last rolled, or the last chips you placed. It deducts from credits, skips anything you cannot afford, and skips bets that are illegal in the current phase (Pass during a point, Come on the come-out, and so on). If there is nothing to put back, the button stays dim and shows a short “Nothing to repeat” note.

      Last roll and history

      After each roll the dice combo (both faces and the total, e.g. 3–4 = 7) shows on the felt above the numbers — not only in the bubble. On a phone that bar stays in view while you scroll the bets. Matching dice are marked Hard. The last-rolls rail sits under Repeat / Roll and shows each combo, not just the total. Big Red never shows seven-dice art (table etiquette).

      Take a bet down

      Use Take down and tap a chip, or drag the chip off the layout (or onto the take-down tray). Same lock rules either way: Pass and Don't Pass stay until they resolve; Come / Don't Come points cannot be picked up. Place, Buy, odds, hardways, and one-roll props refund to credits when they come off.
