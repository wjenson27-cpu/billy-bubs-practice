      How this machine works

      One cabinet, two rule sets

      This is a bubble-craps practice screen. Toggle Regular or Crapless without leaving the machine. Switching rules picks up the table and keeps your credits. Rolls are always fair random 1–6, like a real bubble machine — there is no dice picker.

      Regular

      Come-out: 7 or 11 wins Pass; 2, 3, 12 are craps (Pass loses). 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 set a point. Then hit that point before a 7. Don't Pass bars 12 (push).

      Crapless

      Come-out 7 wins Pass. 2, 3, 11, and 12 are points along with 4–6 and 8–10. No automatic craps. Don't Pass / Don't Come are not offered. Place 2 / 3 / 11 / 12 are available.

      Low Rolls / High Rolls / Roll ’Em All — practice pays

      Always working. Numbers count after you post. A 7 before the set completes loses. You may take them down.

      • Low Rolls — 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 before 7. 31 FOR 1 ($31 returned per $1, includes stake)
      • High Rolls — 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 before 7. 31 FOR 1
      • Roll ’Em All — every total except 7 before a 7. 156 FOR 1

      Fixed practice schedule, not a casino progressive. Real machines vary.

      Hard Way All Day (side wager)

      Lives between Come and Don't Come. Separate from the single Hard 4/6/8/10 bets. Wins only if Hard 4, Hard 6, Hard 8, and Hard 10 all appear (matching dice) before a 7. Soft/easy 4, 6, 8, or 10 do not count and do not lose the bet. A 7 before the set is complete loses. 165 FOR 1 ($165 returned per $1, includes stake → $164 profit). Dealer Envy is not offered in this practice build.

      Single hardways, Horn, C, and E

      Hardways sit left in a 2×2 (Hard 6 & 10 over Hard 4 & 8). Center is Big Red (any 7, no seven-dice art — table etiquette). Horn legs are the right 2×2. Pass Line and Don't Pass run along the bottom of the felt. Hard 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 stay up until a hard win (paid, bet stays), a 7, the easy way, or you take them down. One-roll props repeat after a win like a machine.

      • Hard 6 / Hard 8 — 9:1 · Hard 4 / Hard 10 — 7:1
      • Big Red — any 7. 4:1
      • Aces (2) / Midnight (12) — 30:1 on the winning dollar
      • Ace-deuce (3) / Yo (11) — 15:1
      • C · Any Craps — 2, 3, or 12. 7:1
      • E · Eleven (Yo) — 11 only. 15:1
      • Horn 4-way — 2, 3, 11, 12. A $4 unit: 2/12 pay 30:1 on the $1 that hits (net +$27); 3/11 pay 15:1 (net +$12). Posted in $4 units.

      Buy vs Place

      Each point number has Buy above and Place below. Both stay up after a hit and lose on 7. Off on the come-out unless you check “Place & Buy work on come-out.”

      • Place — 4/10 = 9:5 · 5/9 = 7:5 · 6/8 = 7:6. Crapless Place 2/12 = 11:2 · 3/11 = 11:4.
      • Buy — true odds: 4/10 = 2:1 · 5/9 = 3:2 · 6/8 = 6:5. Crapless Buy 2/12 = 6:1 · 3/11 = 3:1.
      • Buy vig is 5% of the buy stake, taken on a win only (not charged when you post, and not charged if the bet loses). Example: $20 Buy 4 pays $40 true odds minus $1 vig → $39 profit; the $20 stays up.
